        The origin

        This is not a startup product.

        Every sauce and rub in this lineup was stress-tested in a real professional kitchen before it ever had a label. It's thirty years of knowing what works, finally available for your kitchen.

        1. Halifax · Age 16

          The drive-through window

          Memorizing the regulars at a Burger King window, calling their orders before they stopped the car. That reaction is the feeling he's chased ever since.

        2. The bar years

          Hospitality over everything

          From bartending to general manager, taking a $400,000 restaurant to $3 million by teaching a team to make every guest feel like the only person in the room.

        3. 2013 · Smak

          Healthy fast food, before it was a thing

          Pre-prepped great ingredients, served in thirty seconds. Late Checkout was built in 2014 and has never been reformulated.

        4. Maison Simons · 8 years

          The meatball that changed everything

          Twenty dishes for sixteen board members. Dish twenty, chocolate beer meatballs, ended the process. Then eight years and seven venues across Canada.

        5. BRGR BRGR · Abbotsford

          Six years of proof

          His chicken strips were the most ordered item on the menu for six consecutive years.

          Questions, answered.

          Is everything really gluten-free?

          Yes. Every sauce and rub in the line is gluten-free and celiac-safe, pH tested, and made in a Vancouver Coastal Health compliant kitchen.

          How does shipping work?

          Everything ships from Vancouver, BC. Shipping is free in Canada on orders over $75, and always included with the Founding 50 set.

          What's the Sauce Ledger?

          It's not a loyalty program. No points, no tiers, no expiry. Every purchase earns a permanent number that shows when you found the brand. Founders hold SB-11 to SB-60, and the public Ledger opens at SB-500.

          Can I pause or skip my Society box?

          Anytime. Pause, skip a shipment, or cancel from your account in a few taps. Boxes ship every 60 days from the day you join.

          What are the allergens?

          Each product lists its allergens on the label and in its details. For example, Foul Play contains sesame, milk, mustard and sulphites, and Late Checkout contains mustard.
